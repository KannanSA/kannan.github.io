# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, research collaborators, and clinic partners who open a personal site to decide whether to write. Inferred from the existing portfolio and the restyle request; the user did not sit a separate interview.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio for Kannan Sekar Annu Radha: software, physics research, education, and a way to email or download a CV.

## Positioning

One person who ships healthcare software, iOS products, and computational-physics research. Not a lab, a studio site, or a model company.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan on a phone or laptop, then email, download a CV, or open GitHub, LinkedIn, or a project link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static Next.js export. Facts already on the page stay factual: roles, dates, organisations, the move from 42% to 71% ICD-9 accuracy, 2.5M+ AR views, 150K downloads, 5,000+ structures, prizes, and the listed stack. Do not invent customers, benchmarks, or employment. The CV path stays `/KSCV7R.pdf`. The mailto composer stays.

## Brand Commitments

The user pinned the visual system to the live https://x.ai/ homepage. The site remains Kannan’s. It does not use the X or xAI marks, their copy, or their product UI.

## Evidence on Hand

Copy and links live in `app/page.tsx`. Mark asset: `public/sakannan-mark.svg`. The CV file is not in this checkout. No testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Keep every claim traceable to the existing record.
- Lead with the work, then the path to write.
- Use a company-site structure without pretending to be a company.
